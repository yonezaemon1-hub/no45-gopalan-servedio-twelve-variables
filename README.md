# The Gopalan–Servedio Square-Root Bound Holds Through Twelve Boolean Variables

Ryutaro Yonezu — Independent Researcher  
Research Paper No.45, unrefereed AI-assisted preprint, version 1.0.

The paper proves the square-root real-degree bound for nonconstant sign-valued Boolean functions on at most twelve variables: the sum of the absolute first-order Fourier coefficients is at most the square root of the real multilinear degree. Consequently, any counterexample requires at least thirteen variables. This finite-dimensional theorem does not restore the general conjecture or restrict the known unbounded violations.
